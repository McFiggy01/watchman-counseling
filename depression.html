When You're Exhausted by Just Getting Through the Day

Depression isn't just sadness. It's numbness. It's disconnection. It's the low energy that makes even small things feel impossible, and slowly withdrawing from people and activities you used to care about. It's also treatable — not by pushing through it alone, but by understanding what's driving it and building a path forward.

What Depression Can Look Like

◆

Low Energy & Difficulty Getting Up

Everything feels heavier than it should. Getting out of bed, answering a text, making food — tasks that used to be automatic now feel like too much.

◆

Losing Interest in Things You Used to Enjoy

Hobbies, relationships, plans — things that used to bring meaning now feel flat or unreachable. This loss of pleasure is one of depression's clearest signs.

◆

Feeling Numb, Not Just Sad

Depression doesn't always look like crying. Sometimes it looks like feeling nothing — empty, hollow, going through the motions without really being present.

◆

Withdrawing From People

Canceling plans, not returning calls, isolating at home. The pull to withdraw is powerful — and it feeds the depression even as it feels like relief.

◆

Negative Self-Talk & Hopelessness

A quiet but relentless voice that says things won't get better, you're not good enough, or what's the point. Depression distorts the way you see yourself.

◆

Changes in Sleep or Appetite

Sleeping too much or too little. Eating too much or not at all. Depression affects the body — and when these patterns set in, they deepen everything else.

The approach

How We Work With Depression

Depression therapy at Watchman Counseling combines Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) with behavioral activation — two of the most evidence-supported approaches for depression. The goal isn't just getting through the week. It's understanding what's maintaining the depression and creating real momentum toward change.

CBT helps you identify and challenge the thought patterns that keep depression in place — the hopelessness, the self-criticism, the all-or-nothing thinking. We don't just talk about feelings; we look at the relationship between thoughts, behavior, and mood, and start interrupting the loops that make depression self-sustaining.

Behavioral activation is about action before motivation — because depression takes away the drive to do things that would actually help. We build small, structured steps back toward engagement, meaning, and connection. Not because you feel like it, but because action creates the feeling that waits on the other side.

Hector L. Figueroa, LCSW brings clinical training and genuine patience to this work. Depression takes time to lift. We'll move at a pace that's sustainable, not one that feels like pressure.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is depression different from just being sad? +
Sadness is a natural emotion that comes and goes in response to life events. Depression is a clinical condition that persists over time, affects multiple areas of functioning, and often isn't tied to a specific cause. When low mood, low energy, loss of interest, or feelings of hopelessness last for two weeks or more and interfere with your daily life, that's worth taking seriously — not pushing through.
Can telehealth therapy help with depression? +
Yes — telehealth therapy for depression is well-supported by research and works as effectively as in-person treatment for most people. For someone whose depression makes it hard to leave the house, telehealth removes a real barrier. Sessions happen from wherever you are in New York State, on a device you already have. The work is the same; the commute isn't.
What if I need medication too? +
Medication and therapy aren't competing options — for many people, they work best together. As a therapist, I can't prescribe, but I work collaboratively with prescribers when that's part of the picture. If medication is something you're considering or already using, we'll factor that into how we approach the work. If you need a referral to a prescriber, I can help point you in the right direction.
