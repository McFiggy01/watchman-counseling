About Us
Growth. Clarity. Support. Compassion.

Dedicated to Your Mental Well-Being

I'm a New York licensed master's-level social worker with over two decades in behavioral health — from substance use clinics in the Bronx to outpatient mental health work with families, teens, and couples. What I do is straightforward: I sit with people while they figure out what's going on, and we build practical tools to make daily life work better.

Your mental health journey is not just a path — it is an ongoing process of growth, strength, and resilience. I'm deeply committed to giving you the tools, guidance, and support to overcome obstacles, develop coping skills, and embrace what's already inside you.

FAQ

Common Questions

Got one not on the list? The chat in the corner is there 24/7.

How much does therapy cost?

Self-pay rates and any sliding scale availability are discussed during the free 15-minute consult. I work out-of-network and provide superbills you can submit to your insurance for possible reimbursement.

Do you take insurance?

I'm out-of-network. Many PPO plans reimburse a portion of out-of-network mental health visits — I provide superbills (itemized receipts with diagnosis and CPT codes) so you can file for reimbursement.

Where are sessions held?

Telehealth via secure, HIPAA-compliant video. I'm licensed in New York State, so clients must be physically located in NY at the time of each session.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Bilingual and bicultural — sessions can run fully in English, fully in Spanish, or a mix.

Is this Christian counseling?

Faith-integrated counseling is available on request — I'm a Chaplain with the NY State Chaplains Task-Force. By default, sessions are clinical and faith-neutral unless you ask for it.

What's the cancellation policy?

24-hour notice to reschedule or cancel. Late cancels and no-shows are charged the full session fee.

I'm in crisis right now — can you help?

Watchman Counseling is not an emergency service. If you're in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911 right now — or go to your nearest ER. Once you're safe, come back and book a consult.

Prioritize Your Mental Health

No commitment. We talk for 15 minutes — what you're dealing with, what kind of support helps, whether I'm the right fit. If I'm not, I'll point you to someone who is.
