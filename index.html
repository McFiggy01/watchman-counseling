About Us
Growth. Clarity. Support. Compassion.

Dedicated to Your Mental Well-Being

I'm a New York licensed licensed clinical social worker with over two decades in behavioral health — from substance use clinics in the Bronx to outpatient mental health work with families, teens, and couples. What I do is straightforward: I sit with people while they figure out what's going on, and we build practical tools to make daily life work better.

Read My Approach

Empower Your Mind,
Unlock Your Potential

Your mental health journey is not just a path — it is an ongoing process of growth, strength, and resilience. I'm deeply committed to giving you the tools, guidance, and support to overcome obstacles, develop coping skills, and embrace what's already inside you.

Personalized Support

Every individual is unique. My tailored approach makes sure you get care that fits your specific needs — and we move at your pace, not a textbook's.

Evidence-Based Methods

CBT, Trauma-Focused CBT, and harm reduction lead the way — not because they're trendy, but because the research is on their side.

Confidential & Judgment-Free

Your privacy is the floor, not the ceiling. HIPAA-compliant telehealth, no judgment, no labels — a real space to be honest about what's actually happening.

How I Work

How I Make a Difference in Your Mental Health Journey

Personalized Therapy Plans

Every journey is different. We build a plan around your goals, your pace, and what you actually want to change — not a generic protocol.

Evidence-Based Toolkit

CBT, Trauma-Focused CBT, harm reduction, group dynamics — methods chosen because the research backs them, not because they're popular.

Compassionate, Direct Care

Privacy is the floor. Honest conversation is the work. No couch theatrics, no jargon — just space to figure out what's actually happening, and what to do next.

Prioritize Your Mental Health

No commitment. We talk for 15 minutes — what you're dealing with, what kind of support helps, whether I'm the right fit. If I'm not, I'll point you to someone who is.
