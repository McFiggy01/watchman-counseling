Meet Hector
Growth. Clarity. Support. Compassion.

Hector L. Figueroa, LCSW

I'm a New York licensed clinical social worker with years of experience in behavioral health — from substance use clinics in the Bronx to outpatient mental health work with individuals, families, and teens. What I do is straightforward: I sit with people while they figure out what's going on, and we build practical tools to make daily life work better.

Faith integration is available on request — sessions are faith-neutral by default.

What Working Together Looks Like

We start by understanding what you're dealing with and what you're hoping to get from therapy. From there, we set goals together and work at a pace that makes sense for you — using practical tools drawn from evidence-based approaches.

Understanding Your Concerns

We take time to get a clear picture of what's happening — not just the symptoms, but the patterns underneath them. That understanding shapes everything we do together.

Setting Goals Together

You set the direction. I bring evidence-based tools — primarily CBT and Trauma-Focused CBT — to help you build real, lasting change at a pace that works for your life.

Practicing Useful Coping Skills

Sessions are practical. You leave with skills and frameworks you can actually use — not just insight, but tools for handling what comes up between sessions.

How I Work

How I Make a Difference in Your Mental Health Journey

Personalized Therapy Plans

Every journey is different. We build a plan around your goals, your pace, and what you actually want to change — not a generic protocol.

Evidence-Based Toolkit

CBT, Trauma-Focused CBT, harm reduction, group dynamics — methods chosen because the research backs them, not because they're popular.

Compassionate, Direct Care

Your privacy is protected. Honest conversation is the work. No jargon, no judgment — just dedicated space to understand what's happening and build a clear path forward.

FAQs

Common Questions

How do I get started? +
Book a free 15-minute consultation using the calendar on this page. We'll talk about what you're dealing with, what kind of support tends to help, and whether we're a good fit. If I'm not the right person, I'll point you toward someone who is.
Do you offer telehealth or in-person sessions? +
All sessions are conducted via telehealth — secure video or phone. This means you can work with me from anywhere in New York State, on your schedule, without a commute.
What are your fees and payment options? +
Contact me for current rates. I accept major credit cards and can provide a superbill for clients seeking out-of-network reimbursement from their insurance plan. Full fees & payment details →
Do you take insurance? +
I am currently a private-pay practice. Many clients with out-of-network benefits can be reimbursed a portion of session costs through their insurance — I can provide a superbill to submit to your plan. Learn how superbills work →
How long does therapy take? +
It depends on your goals and what you're working through. Some people find meaningful progress in 8–12 sessions; others benefit from longer-term support. We'll check in regularly and adjust the plan as your needs change.
Is everything I share confidential? +
Yes. Everything shared in therapy is protected under HIPAA and New York State law. There are limited legal exceptions — such as imminent risk of harm — which I'll explain clearly at the start of our work together.
