90 Days of College

Nobody Here Knows You — A Christian Devotional for Graduating Seniors and Freshmen

It's late August. You help them carry boxes into a dorm room. Then you drive away. Somewhere around week three, when the money is gone and the roommate is impossible and they cannot get out of bed, your kid is going to decide whether to tell anyone. Most decide not to. This ninety-day devotional was written for that exact semester — by a licensed social worker who has spent twenty-five years in the room with people at their worst, and who watched his own children go away to school.

About Watchman Press

Watchman Press publishes practical, faith-rooted resources for mental health, recovery, and the harder parts of being human. Its books are written by Hector Figueroa, LCSW — a behavioral health therapist with more than two decades walking alongside people in recovery, and a man in long-term recovery himself.
