Using Your Insurance

You may be able to get reimbursed even if we are not in your network.

Do you take insurance?
Please contact us directly to discuss insurance options. We are happy to help you understand what may be available to you.
What is a superbill?
A superbill is an itemized receipt that includes everything your insurance company needs to process a reimbursement claim. If you have out-of-network benefits, you can submit a superbill directly to your insurer and get a portion of the session cost reimbursed. Many clients with PPO plans recover 50–80% of their session fees this way. We provide superbills upon request.
How do I find out if I have out-of-network benefits?
Call the member services number on the back of your insurance card and ask: "Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits?" and "What is my out-of-network deductible and reimbursement rate for outpatient psychotherapy?" Most calls take under 10 minutes.
What about HSA or FSA accounts?
Yes — therapy is an eligible expense under most Health Savings Accounts and Flexible Spending Accounts. We can provide documentation for reimbursement on request.

No Surprises Act — Good Faith Estimate

Under the No Surprises Act, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of the expected cost of your care before your first session. This estimate will be provided to you at the start of treatment and upon request at any time.
